Did you know that, according to legend, a sea demon is the reason the Maldives became Muslim?
Long ago, every month, a ship of glowing lights rose out of the sea near Malé.
The islanders called it Rannamaari, and it wanted a sacrifice.
So each month, they left a young girl alone in a temple by the shore.
By morning, she was gone.
Then, in eleven fifty-three, a traveller from Morocco arrived. His name was Abu al-Barakat.
He heard a family crying. Their daughter was next.
So he took her place.
That night, he sat alone in the temple and recited the Quran, louder and louder.
The lights rose from the sea, came closer, and closer, and then stopped.
Rannamaari turned around and sank back beneath the waves.
The king was so amazed that he, and the whole island, embraced Islam.
And the traveller's tomb is still in Malé today.
